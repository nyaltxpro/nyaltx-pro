## Milestone-Based Development Roadmap

Development is staged so product claims, regulatory analysis and live market activity remain clearly separated.

### Stage 1 — Validate the Foundation

Document live NYALTX capabilities, confirm entity ownership and product architecture, and prepare a technical demonstration.

### Stage 2 — Design Governa Securities Workflows

Prototype issuer onboarding, token issuance, governance, transfer restrictions and transfer-agent interfaces; commission smart-contract and security reviews.

### Stage 3 — Select Regulatory Pathway

Engage securities counsel and prospective BD/ATS/TSV partners; produce a written analysis of applicable registrations, exemptive conditions, product scope, costs and control responsibilities.

### Stage 4 — Build Permissioned Trading Pilot

Develop a controlled AMM proof of concept, identity/eligibility checks, surveillance, halt integration, disclosure and recordkeeping; **do not conduct live securities trading before all prerequisites are met**.

### Stage 5 — Institutional Commercialization

Secure issuer and infrastructure partnerships, launch approved services, measure adoption and recurring revenue, and expand only as regulatory permissions and operating capacity allow.
