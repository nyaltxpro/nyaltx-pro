## Governa: Proposed Tokenization and Governance Layer

Governa is positioned as the planned infrastructure layer supporting compliant digital securities administration. Its proposed scope includes token issuance workflows, issuer and security reference data, holder eligibility and transfer restrictions, governance and voting events, corporate actions, reporting, and integration with appropriately registered transfer agents and professional advisers.

These are product objectives; this paper does not represent that Governa currently acts as a registered transfer agent, broker-dealer, custodian or operating securities venue.

### Proposed Functional Architecture

- **Issuer onboarding and security definition:** entity verification, capitalization data, rights and offering restrictions.
- **Tokenization workflow:** approved contract templates, permission controls, issuance and burn/reconciliation events.
- **Securities administration:** transfer-agent connectivity, authoritative ledger reconciliation, audit trails and corporate actions.
- **Governance:** voting entitlements, notices, record dates, permissions and reporting.
- **Integration APIs:** securely expose verified issuer and security metadata to NYALTX discovery and a future authorized trading layer.
