## 11. Principal Risks

**Regulatory risk:** exemptive relief is temporary, conditional, narrow and subject to change. **Execution risk:** licensing, partnerships and production technology may be delayed or unavailable. **Market risk:** tokenized securities may lack issuers, investors or sustainable liquidity. **Smart-contract and cyber risk:** code flaws, wallet compromises, key management failures and chain outages. **Operational risk:** custody, settlement, records, trading halts, sanctions and market surveillance. **Commercial risk:** revenue assumptions, customer acquisition, liquidity-provider economics and capital requirements may not be achieved.
