## Executive Summary

The capital-markets opportunity is not simply to create another crypto token listing site. It is to connect issuer identity and disclosures, legally valid digital securities records, investor-facing discovery, and—when authorized—secondary-market liquidity. NYALTX can provide a familiar institutional interface to blockchain-native financial infrastructure.

The proposed ecosystem comprises three complementary layers:

1. **NYALTX.pro** — the discovery and investor-awareness layer
2. **Governa.pro** — the proposed issuance, recordkeeping and governance infrastructure
3. **A future regulated or qualifying exempt trading venue** with permissioned automated market maker (AMM) liquidity pools

These layers may be operated by different appropriately authorized entities or partners.
