## 1. Executive Summary

The capital-markets opportunity is not simply to create another crypto token listing site. It is to connect issuer identity and disclosures, legally valid digital securities records, investor-facing discovery, and—when authorized—secondary-market liquidity. NYALTX can provide a familiar institutional interface to blockchain-native financial infrastructure.

The proposed ecosystem comprises three complementary layers: (i) NYALTX.pro, the discovery and investor-awareness layer; (ii) Governa.pro, the proposed issuance, recordkeeping and governance infrastructure; and (iii) a future regulated or qualifying exempt trading venue with permissioned automated market maker (AMM) liquidity pools. These layers may be operated by different appropriately authorized entities or partners.
