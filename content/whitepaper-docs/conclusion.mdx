## Conclusion

NYALTX’s next phase is a focused capital-markets infrastructure strategy: retain the existing DeFi awareness platform, develop Governa as the proposed tokenization and governance layer, and pursue a legally supportable route to permissioned trading of eligible tokenized securities. The September 17 SEC order provides a concrete regulatory reference point for evaluating a TSV-based AMM model. The investment case depends on disciplined execution, verifiable technology, experienced regulated partners and transparent milestones—not on a claim of existing authorization.

> The case rests on disciplined execution, verifiable technology, regulated partners and transparent milestones — not on a claim of existing authorization.
