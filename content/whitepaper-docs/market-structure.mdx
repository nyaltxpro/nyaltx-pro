## Proposed Market Structure and AMM Trading

NYALTX intends to assess three potentially complementary pathways:

| Path | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **A** | Partnership with an existing SEC-registered broker-dealer and, where relevant, FINRA-member ATS operator |
| **B** | Acquisition or establishment of a registered broker-dealer and possible ATS operation subject to applicable approvals and rules |
| **C** | Development or partnership with a qualifying TSV operating under the September 17 conditional exemption |

No path should be treated as automatic or interchangeable.

The proposed AMM would be permissioned, with participant eligibility checks, tokenized-stock qualification, liquidity provider controls, auditable smart contracts, market surveillance, transaction records, incident response and halt propagation. The precise architecture—custody, settlement, cash leg, stablecoin use, pricing controls and on/off-chain reconciliation—would be designed with securities counsel and authorized market participants.

### Target Transaction Lifecycle — Conceptual

1. An issuer and its advisers establish legally valid securities rights and recordkeeping arrangements.
2. Governa or its authorized partners support token issuance and governance administration.
3. NYALTX publishes verified discovery and disclosure information without implying exchange listing approval.
4. Eligible participants access an authorized trading venue or qualifying TSV.
5. A permissioned AMM facilitates permitted transactions, with surveillance, records, settlement and transfer controls.
