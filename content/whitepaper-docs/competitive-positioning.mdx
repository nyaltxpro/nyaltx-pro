## Competitive Positioning

NYALTX’s proposed differentiation is a combined issuer-to-investor workflow: existing Web3 discovery, Governa’s planned securities administration, and a future compliant secondary trading interface. The thesis is integration and distribution, not an assertion that the company has already solved regulatory licensing, custody, market-making capital or institutional adoption.
