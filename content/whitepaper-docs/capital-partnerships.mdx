## 10. Strategic Capital and Partnership Priorities

NYALTX seeks discussions with registered broker-dealers, ATS operators, prospective TSV operators, transfer agents, market makers, institutional technology providers, securities counsel and strategic capital partners. A financing program would prioritize legal structuring, technical development, audits, compliance personnel, infrastructure integration, and working capital. Funding size, valuation, ownership, use-of-proceeds allocation and financial projections should be added only after management approval and diligence.

### Institutional diligence package to prepare

- Corporate structure, beneficial ownership, intellectual-property ownership and Governa/NYALTX relationship.
- Demonstrable platform functionality, user and usage metrics, technology architecture, code audits and security controls.
- Counsel memorandum covering TSV eligibility, BD/ATS alternatives, tokenized-stock rights and transfer-agent responsibilities.
- Operating budget, milestones, capital requirements, potential partners and risk management plan.
