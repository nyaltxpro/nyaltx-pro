## 7. Business Model and Revenue Opportunities

Potential revenue streams are illustrative and contingent on product completion, customer contracts, licenses, and regulatory analysis:

- **Discovery and issuer awareness:** premium profiles, sponsored visibility, media and content packages.
- **Governa enterprise software:** SaaS licensing, onboarding, governance modules, data integrations and administration services.
- **Issuer implementation:** technical tokenization, workflow configuration and professional-service integrations where permitted.
- **Market infrastructure:** data services, connectivity and authorized venue-related charges, if legally permissible.
- **Strategic partnerships:** white-label technology and integration services for registered intermediaries and service providers.

Transaction-based compensation, securities brokerage activity and exchange/ATS fees may require specific registration or exemptive authority. The model should not assume such revenue until counsel confirms the applicable framework.
